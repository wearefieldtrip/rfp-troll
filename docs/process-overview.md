# RFP Troll: process overview

Five steps. Each ends with a team decision or a hand-off. Pass is possible at any step, always with a reason.

| Step | Team does | App does | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Evaluate** | Uploads the RFP | Creates the profile and Drive folder (client name), pulls dates, budget and the Questions tag, runs the fit review against the rubric | Decision: Move to Questions (or Proposal if questions aren't allowed) · Pass |
| **2. Questions** | Reviews the questions doc, sends it, marks it sent, adds the answers | Builds the questions doc from the template; AI suggested questions inserted as Google Docs suggestions; reads answers, flags changed facts, re-scores, summarizes other agencies' questions | Decision: Move to Proposal · Pass |
| **3. Proposal** | Writes the proposal and finishes the estimate | Creates the **proposal as a Google Doc** from the template, with sections in the RFP's required order; creates the estimate sheet; adds the RFP's requirements for each section as comments; offers matching content from past proposals as suggestions with their source | Hand-off: Ready for review |
| **4. Review** | Fixes what's flagged, requests another pass | Checks the proposal Doc against every RFP requirement (sections, page limits via PDF export, forms, scoring criteria). Comments only, never edits | Hand-off: Submitted |
| **5. Outcome** | Records finalist / pitch, then won or lost with a reason | Saves the outcome to history | Closed: Won · Lost · Passed |

## Statuses

Status is the step name plus a word or two: Evaluate · Reading, Evaluate · Ready for decision, Questions · Drafting, Questions · Sent, Questions · Answers in, Proposal · Drafting, Proposal · In review, Submitted · Waiting, Submitted · Shortlisted, Closed · Won / Lost / Passed.

## Formats

- Proposals are **Google Docs**.
- The app does **not** create pitch decks. If Fieldtrip is invited to pitch, the team builds the deck outside the tool; the app records that a pitch happened.
- No AI-written creative copy. The app places structure, requirements and Fieldtrip's own past content; the team writes.
