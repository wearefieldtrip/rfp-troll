# Fieldtrip RFP Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 3, then add the four scores for a total out of 12. The total determines the next step.

## A. Mission and Segment Alignment

- **3 – Excellent:** Direct alignment with a core Fieldtrip segment. The mission is clear, and we can immediately see how to multiply it.
- **2 – Good:** Adjacent segment or slightly vague mission, but highly positive social impact.
- **1 – Fair:** Weak mission alignment or commercial client with a cause-marketing angle.
- **0 – Poor:** No mission alignment or purely transactional commercial project.

## B. Budget and Value Health

- **3 – Excellent:** Budget is explicitly stated and matches the requested scope. Allows for healthy margins without cutting corners.
- **2 – Good:** Budget is unstated but historical data or client type suggests a healthy standard range for this scope.
- **1 – Fair:** Budget is low or tight, requiring scope compromises. High risk of over-functioning to deliver quality.
- **0 – Poor / Auto No-Go:** Unrealistic budget or free/spec work required in the proposal itself.

## C. Scope and Relationship Boundaries

- **3 – Excellent:** Scope is tightly defined with clear deliverables. Client values strategic expertise. Fieldtrip has direct access to decision-makers.
- **2 – Good:** Scope is mostly clear but has some open-ended elements. Access to decision-makers is limited to written Q&A.
- **1 – Fair:** Vague scope that invites "do everything" expectations. No access to decision-makers.
- **0 – Poor:** Extremely bloated scope designed to get maximum labor for minimum pay. Vague or shifting requirements.

## D. Timeline and Capacity Feasibility

- **3 – Excellent:** Proposal deadline is at least 3 to 4 weeks away. Project timeline meets or exceeds the standard benchmark for the scope type.
- **2 – Good:** Proposal deadline is tight (1 to 2 weeks) but we have relevant templates. Project timeline is 20 to 30 percent shorter than the benchmark.
- **1 – Fair:** Proposal deadline requires significant effort to meet. Project timeline is 40 to 50 percent shorter than the benchmark.
- **0 – Poor:** Project timeline is so compressed relative to scope that delivering quality work without burning out the team is not feasible.

## Decision Matrix

- **10 – 12 · Definite Go:** Proceed to proposal drafting. Highly aligned, profitable, and manageable.
- **7 – 9 · Conditional Go:** Proceed only if we can negotiate scope, clarify the budget, or secure direct access.
- **4 – 6 · Likely No-Go:** Decline unless there is a strong strategic reason, such as a foot-in-the-door with a massive long-term client.
- **0 – 3 · Definite No-Go:** Politely decline. Pursuing this trades team bandwidth for low-margin, high-stress work.
